Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  |  Effective upon use of OPSBOT

01

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information OPSBOT ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use the OPSBOT Discord bot, why we collect it, how it is stored and protected, and what choices you have regarding that information.

This policy is separate from our Terms of Service, which governs acceptable use of OPSBOT. Both documents apply to your use of the bot, and this policy should be read alongside it.

By adding OPSBOT to a Discord server, or by using OPSBOT's commands as a member of a server where it is installed, you acknowledge that the data practices described here will apply to you.

02

Information We Collect

OPSBOT collects the minimum data necessary to operate its moderation, blacklist, tracking, and analytics features. This falls into the following categories:

Discord identifiers

Data pointPurpose
Guild (server) IDStore per-server configuration and module settings
Channel IDsRoute logs, alerts, and reports to configured channels
User IDs (members, staff, moderators)Attribute warnings, notes, bans, quota tracking, and event attendance
Role IDsPermission checks for command access and module gating

Roblox-related data

Data pointPurpose
Roblox Group ID(s)Group rank management and group info retrieval via Roblox OpenCloud
Roblox usernames / user IDsBlacklist matching, EXP tracking, division rostering, and verifying that a Discord member corresponds to a real, specific Roblox account
Discord-to-Roblox account verification recordsConfirming a member's claimed Roblox identity before granting verified roles or group-linked permissions
Roblox account age / join dateFlagging newly-created ("alt") accounts during verification for anti-evasion and blacklist enforcement
Roblox group rank / membership statusConfirming eligibility for verified roles and division-specific permissions

Division & moderation records

  • Division names, official status, and configuration metadata
  • Warnings, notes, mutes, kicks, and ban records issued through OPSBOT, including the acting moderator's ID and a timestamp
  • Event records: host ID, event type, attendance counts, and timestamps
  • Aggregate metrics: message counts, member counts, EXP totals, and weekly analytics snapshots

Information we do not intentionally collect

  • Passwords, payment information, or Discord/Roblox account credentials
  • Private messages sent outside servers where OPSBOT is installed
  • Persistent message content (see Section 05)
  • Precise geolocation, device identifiers, or IP addresses beyond what Discord's API itself exposes for standard bot operation
03

How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to operate, maintain, and improve OPSBOT's features:

  • Enforcing the SEA blacklist by cross-referencing Roblox identifiers against configured blacklist sheets
  • Verifying that a Discord account belongs to a specific, legitimate Roblox account before granting verified roles, division access, or group-linked permissions
  • Detecting newly-created or suspicious Roblox accounts ("alts") during verification to prevent blacklist evasion
  • Tracking division EXP, member counts, and event attendance for reporting purposes
  • Applying moderation actions (bans, kicks, mutes, warnings) requested by authorized server staff
  • Generating weekly division analytics reports
  • Diagnosing bugs, monitoring uptime, and improving bot performance
  • Responding to support requests and data deletion requests

We do not use collected data for advertising, do not build cross-server behavioral profiles for marketing purposes, and do not sell data to any third party under any circumstances.

04

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection law (such as the EU/UK GDPR) requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on the following:

  • Legitimate interest — operating core moderation, blacklist, and tracking functionality that server administrators have explicitly enabled
  • Consent — given by a server administrator when installing OPSBOT and by members who continue to participate in a server using it
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law

If you are a member of a server (rather than the administrator who installed OPSBOT), the server owner is generally the data controller for records like warnings and bans, and OPSBOT operates as a data processor on their behalf.

05

Message Content Handling

OPSBOT's message logging module accesses message content transiently and only in servers where the feature has been explicitly enabled by an administrator.

Message content is read in-memory, forwarded to your server's configured log channel, and then discarded. OPSBOT does not write raw message content to a database, and does not retain a searchable archive of message history.

Metadata derived from messages — such as counts used for analytics — may be retained in aggregate form, but the underlying message text is not stored.

06

Data Sharing & Third Parties

OPSBOT integrates with a limited set of third-party services in order to function. Data is only shared with these services to the extent necessary for the corresponding feature to work:

  • Discord — all bot functionality operates on Discord's platform; see Discord's Privacy Policy
  • Roblox OpenCloud API — used for group rank management, group info retrieval, and account verification checks; see Roblox's Privacy Policy
  • Google Sheets API — used in read-only mode to pull blacklist and division tracker data from sheets your administrators configure and control

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies outside of what is described above. We may disclose limited information if legally compelled to do so by valid legal process, or to protect the safety of users, provided such disclosure does not conflict with applicable law.

07

Data Retention

We retain data for as long as OPSBOT remains active in a server, and for a limited period afterward in case the server chooses to re-add the bot:

  • Server configuration and module settings are retained while OPSBOT is installed, and for up to 90 days after removal
  • Moderation records (warnings, notes, bans) are retained for as long as the server keeps them, to preserve moderation history and accountability
  • Event, EXP, and analytics data are retained to support historical reporting, unless deletion is requested
  • Message content is not retained at all (see Section 05)

Administrators or affected users may request earlier deletion at any time by contacting us — see Section 09.

08

Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data collected through OPSBOT, including access controls limiting which systems and personnel can reach production data, and secure handling of API credentials used to connect to Discord, Roblox, and Google Sheets.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you provide information through OPSBOT at your own risk.

09

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding data associated with you, including the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legitimate moderation or record-keeping needs of the server you're in
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent, by leaving servers that use OPSBOT or asking an administrator to remove the bot

To exercise any of these rights, contact support@opsbot.link. Server-wide configuration and moderation-record deletion requests should generally come from a server administrator, since that data is tied to the server's own moderation history; individual members can still request deletion of data specifically about them (e.g., their own warning notes) where this does not conflict with a server's legitimate moderation needs.

10

Children's Privacy

OPSBOT is intended for use within Discord servers and is subject to Discord's Terms of Service, which require users to meet Discord's own minimum age requirement. OPSBOT does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age required by Discord or by applicable local law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in violation of this policy, contact us at support@opsbot.link and we will take reasonable steps to investigate and delete such information.

Because OPSBOT is used within communities organized around a Roblox game, some server members may be minors under applicable law even where they meet Discord's baseline age requirement. We limit the data we collect to what's operationally necessary (Discord/Roblox identifiers and moderation records) and do not use this data for profiling, advertising, or any purpose beyond the moderation and tracking features described in this policy.

11

International Data Transfers

OPSBOT may process and store data on servers located in different countries than the one you reside in. By using OPSBOT, you understand that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in a country that may have different data protection laws than your own.
